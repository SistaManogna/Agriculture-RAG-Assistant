AGRICULTURE RAG DATASET
========================

This dataset contains 8 educational PDF documents designed for a student RAG project.

Files:
1. Soil Health Management
2. NPK Fertilizer Management
3. Irrigation and Agricultural Water Management
4. Integrated Pest Management
5. Crop Rotation and Agricultural Diversification
6. Precision Agriculture and Digital Farming
7. Climate-Smart Agriculture
8. Organic and Sustainable Farming Practices

Good test questions:
- How can soil health be improved?
- What are the roles of N, P and K?
- Compare drip and sprinkler irrigation.
- How does integrated pest management reduce unnecessary pesticide use?
- How can crop rotation affect pests and nutrient management?
- How can precision agriculture support fertilizer decisions?
- What is climate-smart agriculture?
- What is the difference between organic and sustainable farming?
- Which practices are useful for improving water-use efficiency?
- Compare fertilizer placement and fertigation.
- What factors should be considered before making a farm recommendation?

RAG metadata suggestion:
For every chunk, store:
    source_file
    page_number
    topic

Important:
These PDFs are educational project data, not official government recommendations.
For real-world farming decisions, consult current local agronomic guidance and soil-test results.
